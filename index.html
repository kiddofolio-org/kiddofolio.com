How it works

No sorting required. Just place the collection in the box—we’ll handle the curation.

The details

Exactly what you’re buying.

No fine print you have to hunt for. Here’s how the service works before you reserve.

What we can accept

  • Each standard Kiddofolio covers one child. Siblings need separate portfolios, or a custom family quote.
  • Standard orders accept flat or low-relief artwork that fits safely in the box we provide.
  • Oversized, bulky, fragile, or three-dimensional work needs a custom quote.

Shipping

  • Standard pricing includes the shipping kit, prepaid inbound shipping, and return shipping within the contiguous United States.
  • Alaska, Hawaii, international destinations, expedited shipping, and unusual shipping requirements are quoted separately.

Collection size

  • Collection tiers are capacity-based. Sending fewer pieces doesn’t reduce the collection-service price.
  • If there are too few usable pieces to fill the website size you chose, we’ll contact you and offer either a smaller finished portfolio or a downgrade with a credit.
  • Downgrade credits are applied to your unpaid balance first; only an excess credit is refunded.

Timeline

  • Standard turnaround is roughly 4–8 weeks after your artwork is received, depending on collection and website size.
  • We give you a more precise estimate once the collection arrives.

Revisions

  • Your first website version includes one consolidated revision round.
  • Artwork swaps are capped at about 10% of the website size you chose: 3 swaps for 25 works, 5 for 50 works, and 10 for 100 works.
  • Factual corrections and minor design notes are unrestricted within that round.

Approval

  • You have 30 calendar days to submit revisions.
  • If we don’t hear from you within 30 days after reminders, the first version is considered approved. We may then finalize the website, collect the remaining balance, and return your artwork.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

How does pricing work?

Your price is two choices added together: a collection service price based on how big the pile is, and a website price based on how many works we feature. Both are shown in the configurator, and the total updates as soon as you pick. A 50% deposit is due today and the balance is due after you approve your first version. Shipping is included within the contiguous U.S.

How many revisions do I get?

Your first website version includes one consolidated revision round. Artwork swaps are capped at about 10% of the website size you chose — 3 swaps for 25 works, 5 for 50 works, and 10 for 100 works. Factual corrections and minor design notes are unrestricted within that round.

What if I don’t respond to the review request?

You have 30 calendar days to submit revisions. If we don’t hear from you within 30 days after reminders, the first version is considered approved. We may then finalize the website, collect the remaining balance, and return your artwork.

Do I have to sort the art first?

No. That’s the whole idea — send the pile exactly as it is. No sorting required. Just place the collection in the box and we’ll handle the curation, choosing the strongest pieces for the website size you picked.

Can you build galleries for more than one child?

Each standard Kiddofolio covers one child. Siblings need their own separate portfolios, or we can put together a custom family quote that keeps everyone together under one site. Request a custom quote and we’ll scope it with you.

Can this be a gift from a grandparent or relative?

Yes — a Kiddofolio gallery makes a thoughtful gift from grandparents, aunts, uncles, or anyone close to the family. It’s also a lovely way for relatives to keep up with what the kids are making, without waiting for parents to send photos along. You can reserve a gallery directly using the pricing above.

What kinds of artwork can I send?

Standard orders accept flat or low-relief artwork that fits safely in the box we provide — drawings, paintings, and paper-based school projects. Oversized, bulky, fragile, or three-dimensional work needs a custom quote.

Who owns the website and artwork?

You do. Kiddofolio does not claim ownership of your child’s artwork. You receive a complete, portable copy of the finished site — online and on a USB drive that ships back with your originals — so the site and its content are fully yours.

Who actually builds the galleries?

Scott handles every gallery personally — this is a hands-on family studio, not an assembly line. Scott has built dozens of websites that together draw well over a million views a month, and brings that same craft to your child’s work. Willa and Gray, our cofounders, weigh in with a child’s eye, but the design, build, quality, and delivery are Scott’s professional responsibility.

Who are the founders?

Kiddofolio is a family company. Scott founded it and runs the professional side — working with your family and handling the design, build, quality, and delivery. His children, Willa and Gray, are cofounders and coowners: they bring a child’s perspective on the work and are learning entrepreneurship, technology, and archiving along the way. The craftsmanship and accountability for your gallery are Scott’s.

Do you show my child’s name on the gallery?

We publish a first name or a nickname — never a last name — so the gallery feels personal without becoming a searchable profile of your child. As the parent or legal guardian, you choose which we use, and you approve it before anything goes live.

How do you protect my child’s privacy on a public gallery?

Galleries are public and shareable by design, and we build them with care about what’s shown. We publish a first name or nickname — never a last name, never pictured faces, never a school or another identifying detail — so the gallery stays anonymous. We review submitted work for identifying details and redact any we find. And you review and approve the finished gallery before it goes live, and can request reasonable revisions.

Can the gallery be kept private?

Kiddofolio galleries are public by design — that’s what makes them easy to send to grandparents and relatives and gives your child a small web presence they can grow into. We build them thoughtfully (see how we protect your child’s privacy above), but we don’t offer private, unlisted, or password-protected galleries.

Do you show other clients’ galleries as examples?

No. Out of respect for our families’ privacy, we don’t show other clients’ galleries as examples — and we’ll never ask to use yours. The two examples on this page belong to our junior co-founders, Willa and Gray.

How is the gallery organized?

We can arrange the gallery by age, school year, calendar year, theme, or collection — whichever makes your child’s growth easiest to see. Where it fits, related works can sit together, like self-portraits across a few ages.

Can I move the site later?

Yes. There’s no lock-in. Your USB drive holds a complete copy of the site in standard website files, so you can self-host, switch providers, or hand it to any developer.

Who pays for the custom domain?

Every Kiddofolio comes with a Kiddofolio subdomain at no extra cost. If you’d rather have your own custom domain, we can register and set it up for you, or connect one you already own — just tell us and we’ll include it in your quote. You own a complete, portable copy of the site either way, so you’re never locked in.

Can we add more artwork to the gallery later?

Yes. Any gallery can be expanded in the future as your child makes more art — a later addition costs a fraction of the original build, since the site and design are already in place. We’ll quote each update based on how much you’re adding.

How does the blockchain preservation option work?

For families who want it, we can also publish the gallery to Arweave — a decentralized network where data is paid for once and kept in place long-term, rather than depending on a single server or subscription. We pair it with an AR.IO name (ArNS), which acts like a web address for that permanently stored version, so it stays easy to find and share. It’s available as an add-on on any Kiddofolio — just ask and we’ll include it in your quote.

Can the gallery be found on Google?

Every gallery ships with sensible search-engine basics built in, so it can be found by name once search engines index it. If you want more, search-engine optimization is available as an add-on — we tune titles, descriptions, structured data, and a sitemap so the gallery is easier to find and shows a clean preview when shared. Search engines set their own timing, so we can’t promise a specific ranking, but the groundwork is done right.

Can I get a printed book of the artwork?

Yes — a printed keepsake book is available as an add-on on any Kiddofolio. To be clear, there are two separate physical things: your original artwork always comes back in a keepsake archival box, and the book is a bound printing of the gallery-scans — a reproduction to keep on the shelf or give as a gift, not the originals. You choose how many pieces to include, and we handle the design and printing. Just tell us you want the book and we’ll include it in your quote with the size and page options.

Is Kiddofolio available outside the US?

Not currently. Because the service includes physical shipping, we’re currently available for US families only.

How long does it take?

Standard turnaround is roughly 4–8 weeks after your artwork is received, depending on the size of your collection and the size of your website. We give you a more precise estimate once the collection arrives.

What happens to the originals?

We return every original piece in a keepsake archival box via tracked shipping, along with a USB drive holding your complete site and files. The originals are always yours — scanning never replaces them.

What’s your refund policy?

Orders are final and the 50% deposit is nonrefundable. We don’t offer cancellations. The remaining 50% is due after you approve your first version and before publication.

Do the prices include tax?

The price you see is the price you pay. We don’t add sales tax at checkout, so your total and deposit are your final amounts — no surprises when you reserve.

What if my collection is bigger or smaller than I estimated?

An estimate is fine and a small overage is okay. If the collection materially exceeds the size you selected, we’ll contact you before proceeding. If there are too few usable pieces to fill the website size you chose, we’ll offer either a smaller finished portfolio or a downgrade with a credit — applied to your unpaid balance first, with only an excess credit refunded. Collection tiers are capacity-based, so sending fewer pieces on its own doesn’t reduce the collection-service price.

Can I ask a question before reserving?

Yes. If you have a question before reserving, contact us — but most families find they can price their Kiddofolio and reserve directly.
